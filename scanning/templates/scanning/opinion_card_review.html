An answer is saved on the card and goes into the text later, when the answers are applied; Undo takes it back until then. ↓ and ↑ move between cards, and the card in the middle of the screen is the selected one. → and ← move down and up its options, and Return chooses the outlined one (Esc leaves the typed box). 1, 2, 3 choose a reading, 0 leaves it blank (the word goes, a gap stays empty), e types the words, s skips a card you cannot answer (it goes back for someone else, and your next batch does not wait on it; u takes it back), u undoes, f opens the card to the whole page (f or Esc comes back). Under Problem: t the whole block is not text (bleed-through from the page behind, a stamp: every word of the block goes), r the page is a bad scan and needs scanning again, x a redaction box here is wrong. A stray mark inside the text, such as a West perpendicular read as "1" or "la", is not a problem: pick the reading without it, or "read nothing here" in a gap.
