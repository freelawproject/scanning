Rendered both inline by scan_process.html and standalone by the process_actions view (fetched by the step-1/step-2 viewers to refresh the buttons in place after a page is marked for or restored from deletion). The OCR (dots.mocr) and detection (YOLO) controls are staff-only and move no scan status, so their state comes from `dots_run` and `yolo_run` -- summaries of the ExternalJob rows (issues #190 and #195) -- rather than from `is_processing`. There is no detection button (#250). The daemon starts the run by itself once per shard set, right after the upload, and this bar only shows its state. "Next: Detect" starts nothing either: it walks to step 2 when detections exist and otherwise explains, with the same text as its title (`detect_message`). The review-1 controls (issue #151) read `page_review_ready` / `page_review_done`, the two #154 statuses. Approving is the gate: it is what reveals "Next: Detect". Open page edits do not hide it (#232): the apply runs after the approval, so a bar that waited for the apply waited for a button it was hiding. "Validate" and "Re-validate" are offered to a legacy scan only (`has_legacy_ocr`). A new-pipeline volume needs no re-run: sharding, the bitonal conversion and dots.mocr are deterministic, so the view refuses one for good and says so.
