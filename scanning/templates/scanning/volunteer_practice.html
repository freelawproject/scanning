Real cards from a volume, one for each thing you will meet. Each works like a card of your own: look at the cut of the page, then pick the reading that matches it, leave the place blank, or type the words. The card then tells you whether that was right, and why. The keys are the same as on your cards: 1, 2, 3 choose a reading, 0 leaves it blank, e types, ↓ and ↑ move between cards. Nothing you do here is saved. On your own cards there is one more key: s skips a card you cannot answer, and it goes back for someone else, so skipping a hard card is better than guessing.
