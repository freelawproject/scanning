Return presses the first button of the card in view, x its no-button (No caption, No bracket, Not text, Take out), s skips it, u undoes, f opens the card to the whole page and back, d draws a new box of any label on the card, n adds a footnote box over a text card's block, e an editor's note box, ↓ and ↑ move between the cards, ← and → move a bracket's box sideways. Drag a box at its edges to resize it, inside it to move it. Click the label of any other box on the crop to move or resize that one instead (Return saves it, Esc leaves it): a card is often answered by pulling a box of the page out to the text. A bracket, a caption or a column box is added where its box lies; a faint bracket box is approved or taken out; an image box is kept, pulled in to the picture, or taken out; an editor's note box is kept, pulled in to the note, or taken out; a block of text in no box is covered by a column box or an editor's note box added over it, by the footnote box beside it grown over it (or one added when none is near), or by the page's own column box pulled over it. Every answer is step 2's own write, with its Undo. A box added here reaches the redactions at the next recompute.
