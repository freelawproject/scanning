Overlays r

  • Overlays Off — draws nothing, so you see the page alone.
  • Bounds — draws the opinion boundaries, one colour for each opinion.
  • Overlays — draws the redaction boxes and the margin strips. You can see through them.
  • Solid — draws the same boxes, filled black or white, as the output has them.

Double click a redaction box or a margin strip to select it. Drag it, change its size, or dismiss it. A dismissal deletes nothing.

Detections

  • Detections on a page label shows or hides the boxes on every page.
  • Double click a box to select it. The handles and the Dismiss button come.
  • Drag the body of the box to move it. Drag a handle to change the size.
  • A box of the model that you move becomes your own box. The old box gets a dismissal.
  • Dismiss takes the box out of the volume. Nothing is deleted, and a new import keeps your choice.
  • Click away from the box to deselect it.
  • When an opinion card waits for an anchor, one click on a case caption box or a key icon box sets the anchor.
  • Draw makes a new box. Redact and Whiteout draw a redaction.

Keys

  • r one step of the overlay cycle.
  • ← → the opinion before or after this one.
  • ↑ ↓ one page up or down.
  • Esc clears the selection.
